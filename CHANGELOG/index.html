    MirrorKit version history¶

    Versions are x.y.z. 1.0.0 is the full public release. Before it, only 0.y.0 versions were released on GitHub; 0.y.z with z above 0 were development builds that shipped together in the next 0.y.0 release.

    Version Changes
    1.0.0 Full public release: the same mirrors as 0.10.0, now in the Unchained mod registry. Licensed under GPL-3.0 from this version (0.10.0 and earlier stay MIT).
    0.10.0 Style and body release (development builds 0.9.1-0.9.2):
    • Two choices replace six switches: Style (True / Floor / Funhouse) and Body (Full body / Head only / Stretch head / Headless / Ghost). The presets use them.
    • BP_Mirror_StretchHead renders at Resolution 1 with its own settings, since the effect needs detail.
    • Maps made with older versions: the old switches (PortalMode, MatchViewFov, ClipBehind, ScreenLevel1, HeadSource, HideBody) are now ignored, so set Style and Body on mirrors that used them without a preset. Preset mirrors and default mirrors need nothing. Add the new enums E_MirrorStyle and E_MirrorBody to the nativization exclusions if you nativize.
    • Efficiency: the view's field of view is measured once per frame for all mirrors instead of in every reflection render.
    • README rewritten around styles, bodies, partners and performance.
    • Network: everyone on a server should use 0.10.0.
    0.9.2 Development build: efficiency: the view's field of view is measured once per frame for all mirrors instead of in every reflection render. No visible change. README rewritten around styles, bodies, partners and performance.
    0.9.1 Development build: two choices replace six switches. Style (True / Floor / Funhouse) replaces PortalMode, MatchViewFov, ClipBehind and ScreenLevel1; Body (Full body / Head only / Stretch head / Headless / Ghost) replaces HeadSource and HideBody. The presets use them; BP_Mirror_StretchHead renders at Resolution 1 with its own settings. Maps made with older versions: the old switches are now ignored, so set Style and Body on mirrors that used them (funhouse, floor, headless, stretch-head or ghost mirrors placed without the presets). Preset mirrors and default mirrors need nothing. New enums E_MirrorStyle and E_MirrorBody (add them to the nativization exclusions if you nativize).
    0.9.0 Partner and performance release (development builds 0.8.1-0.8.9):
    • Partners as a setting: facing mirror pairs render two exact levels (their own reflection and the partner's inside it); Depth is gone. Partners (on by default; no effect without a Partner) in the map settings, profiles and mirrors; servers and players switch it with ?MirrorPartners= / -MirrorPartners=, a server can forbid it with ?MirrorNoPartners. New Omega preset = Ultra plus partners; every other preset turns partners off. InfiniteTail continues a tunnel past the second level.
    • Pair checks: a mirror whose Partner does not point back, or that gets Partners differently, shows a red box in the editor and logs a warning; in play the pair falls back to two independent mirrors.
    • Reflection size follows the screen: each reflection is sized to the part of the screen it covers, never above Resolution or what the game renders (screen x resolution scale). Small and distant mirrors cost less; large partner walls get a sharper inner reflection.
    • Smoother partners: only the reflection levels you can see are rendered, and the inner reflection renders at half rate on a frame of its own. Measured on Mirror Maze: partners now cost about 7 % FPS (was about 6 % on the average and 12 % on the 1 % lows in 0.8.1, on a slower base).
    • FocusExemptFromBudget (on): off lets the capture budget slow nearby mirrors too (?MirrorFocusExempt=0).
    • Maps built for 0.8.0 or earlier: partnered mirrors now render as a two-level tunnel by default (set Partners off on them to keep the old single reflection), Depth 3+ shows two levels, and a mirror with a Partner needs 5 cm clear behind its face (was Depth + 3 cm, 4 cm at Depth 1).
    • Network: hosts on 0.9.0 send new options (MirrorPartners, MirrorNoPartners, MirrorFocusExempt) that 0.8.0 clients ignore. Everyone on a server should use 0.9.0.
    0.8.9 Development build: new FocusExemptFromBudget switch (on by default, as before): off lets the capture budget slow focused (nearby / central) mirrors too. Mirror, profile and map settings; ?MirrorFocusExempt= / -MirrorFocusExempt=; presets keep the map's choice.
    0.8.8 Development build: efficiency clean-up: reflection sizes are re-checked at most 10 times a second (at once after a partner change), so a reflection may stay slightly soft for up to 0.1 s when it suddenly needs more detail; the screen size and resolution scale are read once per frame for all mirrors instead of by each mirror; per-level loops replaced by direct steps.
    0.8.7 Development build: a partnered mirror renders its inner reflection at half its update rate and on a frame of its own, preferably on a frame where neither it nor its partner rendered yet (if none comes before its next update, it renders with that update), so a pair you look at mostly costs one render per frame instead of two. Front reflections keep their full rate.
    0.8.6 Development build: reflection sizes are capped at the size the game actually renders (r.ScreenPercentage, the in-game resolution scale), not just the screen: at 50 % a full-screen mirror stops at half the screen. DLSS keeps the screen-size cap. Mirrors whose partner pair is not live free their unused inner-reflection memory.
    0.8.5 Development build: pair checks: a mirror whose Partner does not point back, or that gets Partners differently from it, shows a red box in the editor and logs a warning; in play the pair falls back to two independent mirrors instead of showing dark glass. Screen-coverage sizing: each reflection's render target follows how much of the screen it covers (level 1: the mirror on screen; level 2: the mirror as seen inside its partner), capped by the screen and by Resolution, in steps of about ×1.4 (grows at once, shrinks after 1 s). Large partner walls get a sharper inner reflection; small and distant mirrors use less memory.
    0.8.4 Development build: Depth removed (more than two levels looked the same); a partnered mirror renders two levels, the second at half size. New Partners setting (on; no effect without a Partner) in the map settings, profiles and mirrors, switchable by servers and players (?MirrorPartners= / -MirrorPartners=, cap ?MirrorNoPartners). New Omega preset = Ultra plus partners; every other preset turns partners off. PerLevelResolutionMultiplier removed. Partnered mirrors need 5 cm behind the face, others 4 cm.
    0.8.3 Development build: one setting, PerLevelResolutionMultiplier (default 0.5), sets how much sharper level 1 is than deeper levels: level N renders at the multiplier to the power N-1 of level 1, never below 1/8. It replaces ResolutionProfile, DeepResolutionScale, ResolutionDropPerLevel and MinimumDepthResolutionScale. Depth 2 looks the same as before; level 3 and deeper are now smaller (level 3: a quarter instead of a half).
    0.8.2 Development build: partnered mirrors render only the reflection levels you can actually see (looking at one mirror of a pair needs only its odd levels and the partner's even levels), so a pair seen from one side costs about half. Partner panels are hidden once instead of before every capture.
    0.8.1 Development build: InfiniteTail setting (map settings, profile or mirror; off by default). Partnered tunnels continue past Depth with the partner's own current reflection instead of ending dark.
    0.8.0 First-person reflection release (development builds 0.7.1-0.7.6):
    • Sheathed weapons show in first-person reflections. The game hides your sheathed items from every camera in first person; MirrorKit now shows your own sheathed weapons, shields and other gear to mirrors only (never in your own view).
    • Put-away items: a weapon or shield you put away in first person (including to fists) appears on its sheath in reflections shortly after the swap settles, instead of only after a view switch.
    • On the reflected body: sheathed items follow the reflected body's pose, so a switched-off shield sits on the reflected arm.
    • Lighter player reflections: hair, beard and helmet follow the reflected body's pose instead of being posed separately, and dye colours are copied once a second (at once when a part changes): about a third of the per-frame work for the reflected player.
    • Maps need no rebuild; no network changes (players on 0.7.0 and 0.8.0 can share a server).
    0.7.0 Security, memory and quality-limit release (development builds 0.6.1-0.6.6):
    • Server options stay private: the server shares only recognized Mirror quality settings with players; every other map option is dropped before it is sent.
    • Safe server presets: players only ever load the five built-in presets because of a server. A host can still use a custom preset: the host loads it and sends only the resulting values, so players do not need its PAK.
    • Separate rate caps: ?MirrorMaxUpdateRate= and ?MirrorMaxFocusRate= next to ?MirrorMaxRate= (both).
    • Off frees video memory: mirrors that start Off never reserve their reflection images, and mirrors switched Off later (a server Off reaching a joining player) release them. Switching back on creates them again.
    • Resolution capped at 2.0 (2560 × 1440) wherever it is set; higher values act as 2.0.
    • Everyone on a server needs 0.7.0: older versions do not understand every setting a 0.7.0 host sends.
    0.6.0 Settings, budget, quality and smoothness release:
    • Settings for many mirrors: map-wide BP_MirrorKitSettings, reusable BP_MirrorProfile data assets (MP_MirrorMaze, MP_HighDetail), per-mirror SettingsSource; details-panel categories and tooltips.
    • New defaults = the schedule measured on MirrorMaze: Resolution 0.5, TargetUpdateRate 90 updates/s, FocusUpdateRate 144 for near/central mirrors, StaggerMode Distance, DisableReflectionAO on, ChecksEveryNFrames 4. Mirrors that kept the old defaults switch to these.
    • Capture budget on its own map actor BP_MirrorKitBudget (off without one): adaptive multiplier, MinUpdateRate, focus distance/angle; per-mirror ExcludeFromBudget.
    • MirrorQuality for players and servers: presets MQ_Off/Low/Medium/High/Ultra (all values written out), -MirrorQuality= and single values on the command line, server values and an opt-in server cap in the map options, shared with clients through BP_MirrorKitNet. MQ_Off shows plain dark glass and is reversible.
    • Smoothness at high FPS: SmoothUpdateInterval (on) steadies the update interval when FPS fluctuates; optional AnchorUpdateCadence; time-based rates round instead of floor; better stagger spread on grid-aligned maps.
    • Faster: one hidden BP_MirrorKitManager per machine runs the FPS smoothing, budget and quality parsing; mirrors cache the camera, their transform and their interval (these changes alone: +3-7 % FPS on a 268-mirror map).
    • Removed: the generated back face (TwoSided, TwinGap, BackPartner, BP_Mirror_TwoSided; place a second mirror behind instead, BP_MirrorTileLive gained BackWall), the off-screen test (OffscreenUpdateEveryNFrames; it cost more than it saved on large maps), the distance throttle and TelemetryMark. Maps that set them still load; the values are ignored.
    • Step-by-step comment boxes inside every kit Blueprint.
    0.5.0 Performance and usability release (developed as internal builds after 0.4.0):
    • Independent mirrors: no pairing needed; one capture per face.
    • Cheaper per-frame work: checks before hidden-list maintenance, cached stand-ins, shared Blueprint functions, bounded loops (behaviour verified identical) and editor comment blocks.
    • Distances measured to the nearest point of the mirror face, centre-first and only when a feature uses them.
    • Exact update-slot formula (FrameCounter + phase) % N.
    • New settings OffscreenUpdateEveryNFrames and DisableReflectionAO; two-sided back faces inherit them.
    • CaptureCount / TelemetryMark diagnostics.
    • README rewritten; map-design guide docs/MAP-OPTIMIZATION.md.
    0.4.0 Front/back partner targeting for two-sided mirrors; spacing safeguards (Depth 1-10, TwinGap minimum, clearance guide); default depth 1
    0.3.0 First public release
